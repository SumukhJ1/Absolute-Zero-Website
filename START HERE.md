# Absolute Zero website

Four folders. Here is what each one is for, in the order you will want them.

---

## To look at the website right now

Open **`1 Preview the site`** and double-click:

### `index.html`

That is it. The whole site opens in your browser and every link works.
Nothing to install, no server, no terminal.

If you would rather run it the way a real server would (slightly more
accurate, and the only way the URLs look like `/outreach/` instead of
`/outreach/index.html`), double-click **`Open the website.command`** in the
same folder instead. It starts a small local server and opens your browser
for you. Close the black Terminal window when you are done.

> The first time you double-click a `.command` file, macOS may refuse it.
> Right-click it, choose **Open**, then click **Open** again. You only have
> to do that once.

---

## The four folders

### `1 Preview the site`
The finished site as plain files, for reviewing on your own machine. This
copy is for looking at only. **Do not upload this folder to a web host**:
the links in it are written for your hard drive, not for a server.

### `2 WordPress theme`
The actual deliverable. `absolute-zero-theme.zip` is the file you upload to
WordPress. `How to install.md` walks through it. The unzipped `absolute-zero`
folder next to it is the same theme, opened out, if you want to read the code.

### `3 Needs your input`
Everything still waiting on you, and two empty folders to drop things into.
`Checklist.md` is the full list: 55 photographs, 12 facts to confirm, and the
Open Access PDFs.

### `4 Source code`
How the site is built, and how to rebuild it after a change. You only need
this if you or someone else is editing the design rather than the content.

### `_TO DELETE - drag me to the trash`
Not one of the four. It holds the older, scattered copies of everything that
were in this folder before it was tidied. Nothing in the four numbered folders
depends on it. Have a look if you like, then drag the whole thing to the
Trash.

## The three questions you are most likely to have

**Can I edit the site without touching code?**
Yes, once the theme is on WordPress. Workshops, the roster, sponsors,
programmes, the Open Access library and the competition record are all
ordinary WordPress content. The outreach numbers, the season names and the
footer line are in Appearance to Customize. See `2 WordPress theme`.

**Where do the photos go?**
`3 Needs your input/Photos go here`. Use the exact filenames from the
checklist and they slot straight in.

**Is this live anywhere?**
No. Nothing has been published. The current azrobotics.org is untouched.
