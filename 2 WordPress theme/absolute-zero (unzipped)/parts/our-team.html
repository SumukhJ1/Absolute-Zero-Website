Joining

FIRST Tech Challenge is for students in grades 7 to 12. You do not need to have built anything before. Roughly half of this roster had never touched a robot when they joined, and the fastest way onto the drive team has always been to show up on outreach days.

If you are in Northern Virginia and want to find out what a season looks like, write to us. If you are somewhere else, we will help you find a team near you or start one.
