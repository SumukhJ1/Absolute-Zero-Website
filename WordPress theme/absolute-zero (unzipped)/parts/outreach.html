Access does not stop at the county line.

Almost everything on this page happens within about thirty miles of Ashburn. One programme does not. Namma Bhoomi is a residential campus in Karnataka, India, run by The Concerned for Working Children, and it educates children who have worked for a living. It has a computer lab and a vocational training institute. What it did not have was robotics.

We send kits and we teach remotely. The instruction has to survive a nine and a half hour time difference and a room we have never stood in, which has made us considerably better at writing things down.

Open Access

Every engineering notebook, every pit design, every build and programming guide we have written, published in full. No sign-up, no email wall, no request form. If it helped us, it is yours.

This exists because of one afternoon in 2018 when a team we had never met explained OnBot Java to us and asked for nothing back. Open Access is what paying that back looks like at scale.
