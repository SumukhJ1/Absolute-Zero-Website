Coaches

Both coaches sit on the board of Robotics for Youth, the nonprofit this team is part of. Neither of them drives the robot.

Coach Dinesh

Software developer, and a board member of Robotics for Youth.

Has coached Absolute Zero since the team’s early seasons. Spends most of a build season asking students the question behind the question rather than answering the first one.

Coach Saji

Software engineer, and a board member of Robotics for Youth.

Joining

FIRST Tech Challenge is for students in grades 7 to 12. You do not need to have built anything before. Roughly half of this roster had never touched a robot when they joined, and the fastest way onto the drive team has always been to show up on outreach days.

If you are in Northern Virginia and want to find out what a season looks like, write to us. If you are somewhere else, we will help you find a team near you or start one.
